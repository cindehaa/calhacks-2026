    How to play

    about 10 seconds
    1. Aim by pointing with your index knuckle, or move the mouse over your half.
    2. Lock the spot: pinch thumb and middle finger, click, or just start talking.
    3. Say (or type) what the troop is: “wizard with fire”, “angry bee swarm”.
    4. Release the pinch, or go quiet, to deploy.
    5. Cancel by dragging off the ring before you let go.
    6. The cost is a surprise. It shows on the troop once it lands.
    7. Keys 1–0 drop the starter troops.
    8. Not what you asked for? Say “that’s wrong” or press X.
